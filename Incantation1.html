*Heroes of Metro Magic*

Project Abstract

Magic is the use of unseen, supernatural, or mysterious powers to make impossible things happen. It is the driver of high fantasy novels, role playing games and epic films, where the unlikliest of heroes overcome dark forces against insurmountable odds. It is of course nearly all fiction, but unfortunately, dark forces are universal. Many in our communities live every day with overhwelming adversity, especially in a city with as many challenges as New York. But joy can still be conjured from the most improbable of places, from the most implausible of people. And in these times, that is nothing short of magical.
